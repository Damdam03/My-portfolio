01 — About

From lecture hall to lab bench to production.

I graduated with a BS in Information Technology from Partido State University in 2026, but the more useful way to describe me is: someone who likes taking a messy real-world problem, building a working system for it, and writing down what actually happened along the way.

That's the throughline across my capstone, my internship, and my side projects — full-stack web development, game development in Godot, data analysis, and academic research aren't separate hats to me. They're the same skill, applied to different materials: understand the problem, build the smallest working version, then make it legible to whoever needs to trust it.

Most recently, I completed a 486-hour internship in the Department of Computational Sciences at Partido State University, working as both a full-stack developer and a research documenter.
